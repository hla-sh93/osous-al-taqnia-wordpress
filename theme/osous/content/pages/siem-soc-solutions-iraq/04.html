Critical Cybersecurity Challenges Facing Iraqi Businesses

Businesses in Iraq face numerous cybersecurity challenges:

  • Increasing sophistication of cyber attacks targeting critical infrastructure and sensitive data
  • Evolving regulatory compliance requirements both locally and internationally
  • Growing attack surface due to digital transformation initiatives and IoT adoption
  • Skills shortage in specialized cybersecurity professionals
  • Cloud security concerns as more organizations migrate to cloud environments
  • Supply chain vulnerabilities that can compromise even well-protected systems
