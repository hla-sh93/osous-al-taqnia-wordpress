A phased approach built for accuracy and continuity

The system is rolled out in structured phases — automation and control first, then reporting, then scale — so operations never stop while accuracy improves.

01

Assess & define requirements

Map existing reconciliation processes, transaction types, volumes, and exception rules.

02

Integrate & map data

Secure automated feeds from ATMs, bill payment platforms, cards, and third-party providers.

03

Configure & automate

Automated matching, variance detection, and reconciliation posting, with defined access controls.

04

Report & monitor

Standardized dashboards, real-time exception tracking, and audit-ready reconciliation records.

05

Roll out by phase

High-volume, high-risk channels first — ATM transactions, then bill payments, then cards.

06

Test & validate

Unit and integration testing, benchmarked directly against manual reconciliation results.
