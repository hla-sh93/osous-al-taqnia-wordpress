About Kaspersky

Kaspersky is a global leader in cybersecurity with more than 25 years of experience protecting over 400 million users and 270,000 businesses worldwide. Its AI-powered platforms defend against malware, ransomware, and advanced cyber threats across endpoints, cloud workloads, and hybrid environments.

Key features include:
- Real-time threat intelligence
- Vulnerability scanning
- Ransomware mitigation
- Proactive analytics for high-risk environments
