Hikvision Solutions Offered by Osous Al Taqnia

IP Cameras

We offer a wide selection of HD IP cameras equipped with motion detection, night vision, and remote access capabilities. Ideal for monitoring city streets, commercial buildings, industrial zones, and critical infrastructure.

Analog Cameras

Reliable analog cameras provide cost-effective surveillance for legacy systems. These are well-suited for small businesses, retail shops, and residential applications, with seamless DVR integration.

DVRs & NVRs (Recording Systems)

Our DVRs support analog camera inputs with local storage and playback, while NVRs offer advanced IP camera recording with smart analytics and cloud backup. Centralized video management enables real-time monitoring and alerting.

Video Management Software (VMS)

Hikvision’s VMS offers a powerful command center to control multi-camera environments. It enables live video streaming, intelligent playback, event detection, and seamless integration with access control systems.

Access Control Systems

Our access control solutions include facial recognition terminals, card-based access readers, and biometric scanners. These systems manage time attendance, restrict entry to high-security zones, and integrate directly with surveillance networks.

Specialized Security Products

For high-risk or specialized needs, we supply thermal imaging cameras for perimeter defense and fire detection, video intercom systems for building entry management, and full smart city/industrial surveillance deployments.
