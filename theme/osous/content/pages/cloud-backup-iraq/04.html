Understanding Cloud Backup vs. Cloud Storage

Enhanced Security and Compliance

Cloud backup provides end-to-end encryption, compliance with Iraq's data protection laws, and robust protection against cyber threats.

Cost Efficiency

Eliminates expenses for on-premises hardware, dedicated IT staff, and physical storage while converting capital expenditure to predictable operational costs.

Business Agility

Enables rapid deployment, seamless scaling, and quicker protection of new workloads to meet changing business demands.

Disaster Recovery

Offers resilience through geographically dispersed data centers, rapid recovery capabilities, and protection against both physical and cyber threats relevant to Iraq's unique challenges.
