About Avigilon Solutions

Avigilon, part of Motorola Solutions, is a global leader in intelligent video surveillance and access control technology. Its systems combine artificial intelligence, high-definition imaging, and cloud-based management to help organizations detect and respond to security events quickly and accurately.

Built for reliability and scalability, Avigilon solutions deliver exceptional image clarity, real-time analytics, and secure remote monitoring. They integrate seamlessly with Motorola CommandCentral, giving users a unified view for improved decision-making and faster incident response.
