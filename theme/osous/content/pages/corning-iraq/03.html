About Corning

Corning is a global innovator in optical communications and glass science, with more than 169 years of experience in material development and advanced networking technology. Its fiber and copper cabling systems power critical infrastructure worldwide—including telecom backbones, data centers, industrial systems, and smart cities.
‍
Corning’s products are known for their superior durability, high transmission capacity, and scalability, making them the ideal choice for businesses, governments, and service providers seeking long-term network performance.
