Corning Solutions Offered by Osous Al Taqnia

Copper Cabling Systems

We offer a wide range of structured copper solutions, including Cat5E, Cat6, Cat6A, and Cat7 cabling options, suitable for modern LAN and voice/data networks. Our inventory includes patch cords, faceplates, keystone jacks, cable organizers, and both 24-port and 48-port patch panels to support comprehensive network infrastructure

Fiber Optic Cabling Solutions

Osous Al Taqnia supplies reliable and scalable Corning fiber solutions for core, access, and distribution layers. These include FTTH cables ranging from 2 to 48 cores in both single-mode and multimode, OM2/OM3/OM4 patch cords, pigtails, and pre-terminated panels. We also provide indoor and outdoor enclosures, breakout boxes, and Micro ODFs to support complex fiber installations.

Connectivity & Deployment Accessories

We stock a full range of deployment tools and accessories to streamline installation and performance. This includes fiber splicing kits, enclosures, and essential tools for structured cabling projects across data centers, campus networks, and telecom environments. Our solutions address backbone, horizontal, and telecom room connectivity needs.
