How it works

From alert to report in five steps

A clear, documented path means nothing depends on one person’s memory.

STEP
01
Rules & scenarios (Monitoring)

The system continuously scans activity based on risk parameters.

STEP
02
Alert (Generated)

A potential risk triggers a flag in the system.

STEP
03
Analyst review (Triage)

An analyst reviews each potential match. A false positive lets the transaction proceed; a true match is stopped, frozen, and escalated.

STEP
04
Case (Investigation)

The full file comes together. Documents, customer activity, and the investigation trail stay in one place, from the first alert to the final decision.

STEP
05
STR (Report filed)

The final report is filed, creating a permanent, compliant record of the investigation.

Beyond core AML

CFT, TBML and fraud on the same platform

The risks that sit around money laundering shouldn’t need separate tools.

Counter-terrorist financing (CFT)

Screen customer names and payments against local and international sanctions lists, including UN Security Council lists. Payments are checked before execution, and every potential match goes to an analyst.

Trade-based money laundering (TBML)

Spot red flags in import and export transactions: over- or under-invoicing, the same shipment financed more than once, phantom shipments, mismatched goods, and listed parties, vessels or ports.

Anti-fraud

Cover internal and external fraud, from unauthorized changes and dormant-account withdrawals to account takeover and phishing, with a risk assessment before each transaction runs: allow, verify or decline.
