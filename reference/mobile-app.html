FAQs

What is the cost of mobile app development in Iraq?

App development costs vary based on complexity, platform, and features. Simple apps start at a lower investment, while complex enterprise apps require a larger budget. Contact us and we'll provide a detailed quote within 48 hours.

How long does it take to build a mobile app?

A basic app typically takes 6–10 weeks. Mid-complexity apps with custom backends take 3–5 months. Enterprise platforms may take longer. We provide a clear timeline after your discovery session.

Do you develop iOS and Android apps?

Yes. We build iOS and Android apps using Flutter — delivering native-level performance on both platforms from a single codebase.

Can you build a cross-platform app with Flutter?

Absolutely. Flutter is our cross-platform framework of choice. It allows us to build a single codebase that runs natively on both iOS and Android, reducing cost and time to market significantly without sacrificing quality or performance.

Do you provide post-launch maintenance and support?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance packages that include bug fixes, OS version updates, feature enhancements, and performance monitoring to keep your app running smoothly after launch.

Can you integrate payments, chat, maps, or ERP systems?

Yes. We integrate a wide range of systems — local and international payment gateways, real-time chat, Google Maps, CRMs, ERPs, and fully custom APIs and backends.
