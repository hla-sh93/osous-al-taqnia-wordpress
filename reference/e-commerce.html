How We Work

Our Ecommerce Development Process

A clear, structured workflow from concept to launch — with full transparency at every step.

STEP
01
Discovery & Planning

We start by understanding your products, target customers, competitors, and business goals. This phase defines your store's architecture, platform choice, feature list, and launch timeline — giving your project a solid foundation before any design begins.

STEP
02
UI/UX Design & Prototyping

Our designers create wireframes and full visual mockups of your store — including product pages, category layouts, cart, and checkout — for your review before development starts. You see exactly what your customers will experience.

STEP
03
Development & Integration

We build your store, configure payment gateways (Zain Cash, Qi Card, COD), integrate shipping systems, set up product catalogues, and connect any third-party tools required — all tested rigorously in a staging environment before going live.

STEP
04
Testing & Quality Assurance

Comprehensive testing across devices, browsers, and checkout flows. We verify every payment method, form, and user journey before your final approval. Once you sign off, we deploy to production and monitor closely during the go-live window.

STEP
05
Deployment & Launch

After launch we remain your technical partner — handling performance optimization, ongoing SEO, feature additions, security patches, and any issues that arise. Your store is never left unsupported.

FAQs

How long does it take to build an ecommerce website?

A basic ecommerce store typically takes 4–6 weeks. Stores with custom features, integrations, and multilingual support can take 8–14 weeks. We provide a clear timeline after your discovery session so you always know what to expect.

What payment gateways are available in Iraq?

We integrate Iraq's most trusted local payment options — Zain Cash, Qi Card, and cash on delivery. We also configure international payment gateways for businesses selling outside Iraq. We advise on the best payment mix for your customers.

How much does an ecommerce website cost in Iraq?

Ecommerce costs vary based on complexity, number of products, platform, and required features. Basic stores start at a lower investment while enterprise builds require a larger budget. Contact us for a detailed, personalized quote.

Can I sell internationally from Iraq?

Yes. We build multi-currency and multi-language stores that allow Iraqi businesses to sell internationally, with international shipping options, currency conversion, and payment methods configured for cross-border commerce.

Do I need hosting and domain services?

Yes. We advise on and set up the best hosting environment for your ecommerce store — fast, secure, and optimized for Iraqi and international visitors. We handle the full technical setup so you can focus on your business.

Which ecommerce platform is best for my business?

It depends on your business size and goals. WooCommerce is ideal for flexibility and Arabic support. Shopify is great for fast launches. Magento suits large enterprises. We recommend the right platform after understanding your specific needs.
